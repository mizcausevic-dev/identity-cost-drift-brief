# Kinetic Gain Embedded

Identity Cost Drift Brief is designed to open an embedded executive-intelligence engagement:

- expose where license overlap, role bloat, guest sprawl, and control duplication are eroding board confidence
- identify which lanes need reclamation, standardization, escalation, or containment before the next review cycle
- convert a broad identity-governance story into one usable cost packet with explicit owners and recovery decisions

This is an operator-facing executive-intelligence surface, not accounting advice, legal advice, financial advice, or a live market system.
