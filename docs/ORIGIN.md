# Origin

Identity Cost Drift Brief exists because executive teams often know the identity stack is getting more expensive before they can point to the exact lane where license overlap, duplicated controls, weak reclamation, or guest sprawl is accumulating. Kinetic Gain needs one reusable identity-cost surface that shows where spend discipline is leaking, where role or review burden is too high, where controls are too duplicative, and what should be tightened before the next board or investor review.
